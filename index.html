ABOUT

Enter Ashen Town: Purgatory, where ancient myths collide with visceral survival horror.

Play as Reika, a bio-terrorism operative thrust into a high-stakes investigation.

Brace yourself for breathtaking action and heart-stopping terror as you unravel the dark secrets of a town lost to history.

STORY

A Silence That Screams A series of chilling disappearances has plagued the remote town of Kisaragi, Japan. Yet, a suffocating silence hangs over the media and local authorities—no reports, no warnings, no traces. The truth only begins to surface when a veteran police officer and his young son visit the town for a peaceful vacation, only for the boy to vanish into thin air.

The Descent into Darkness Driven by duty and personal ties, Reika, an elite bio-terrorism operative and colleague of the officer, arrives to investigate. What she discovers is far more sinister than a simple kidnapping. Kisaragi is a place where modern bio-threats merge with forbidden ancient rituals and spreading occultism.

Face the Nightmare As Reika, you must navigate a web of terrifying conspiracies to rescue the child and expose the darkness before it consumes the world. Will you solve the mystery and survive the haunting secrets of this forgotten town, or will you become just another shadow in the mist?
